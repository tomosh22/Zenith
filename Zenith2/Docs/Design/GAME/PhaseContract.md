# GAME design — the frame-phase contract and the lifecycle commands

This file is part of the GAME design family; the entry document is `Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME.md`. It specifies what the GAME area does as the owner of the frame-phase and authority contract (Z2-D-080) and of the lifecycle commands: contract stewardship and change control, the phase placement of GAME's own systems, the authority census that feeds the D-PHASES refinement (open, Stage 1), the refinement candidates and the ruling procedure, the lifecycle command contract matrix with its per-command tests, the five cross-system scenario tests, and the three budget rows that hang off the phase contract. The contract itself is `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/FramePhases.md` and `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/LifecycleCommands.md`; this file never restates a tagged bullet of those files, it adds what a worker needs on top of them.

### Change control

1. The phase list (twelve phases, in order) is settled by Z2-D-080 and is not changed by the D-PHASES refinement; the refinement may change what a phase owns, which systems may run in it, the default precedence, and a lifecycle command's publication point ([Z2-N-ARCH-471], the D-PHASES entry of `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` → "Open decisions").
2. Any change to `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/FramePhases.md` → "The twelve phases" or `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/LifecycleCommands.md` → "Lifecycle command summary" after the D-PHASES ruling is made by a ticket that appends its own decision record `Z2-D-NNN` to `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` in the same diff (Z2-D-085), names D-PHASES or the ruling's ID in its Decisions line, and lists every consumer area of the row it changes from the census (§ "Authority census").
3. A new component property's authority is chosen by the area that registers it and recorded in that area's design document; the census ticket and every subsequent diff that registers a property add the property's row to `Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/AuthorityCensus.md` (§ "Census format") in the same diff, so the census stays complete.
4. A component that needs two direct writers in one phase declares an arbitration rule ([Z2-N-ARCH-484]); an area that wants a precedence other than the default lists its order in its registration and records the reason in its design; GAME reviews it through the census row (column "Arbitration").

## Phase placement of GAME systems

GAME registers three per-world nodes, all of them the Gameplay module's (ZenithRuntime's world host registers no node: it runs in the frame prologue and at the tick boundary through its publication handler); their phases follow the ownership chain of `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/FramePhases.md` → "The twelve phases": requests recorded by scripts in phase 2 land at sync point 2, physics and animation settle in phases 3 to 6, and the gameplay rules and camera direction run once the poses are final and before world transforms are propagated in phase 8.

| Node (`m_szName`) | Owner | Phase | Affinity | Writes directly (authority = this phase) | Reads | Records | Ordering |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Gameplay.WorldEvents` | ZenithGameplay | 1 Input | `JOB_AFFINITY_ANY` | nothing | resource `Zenith.Runtime.WorldState` | the event `Zenith.Gameplay.WorldStarted` (first tick only) | none |
| `Gameplay.Rules` | ZenithGameplay | 7 AI and navigation | `JOB_AFFINITY_ANY` | every property of `Zenith.Gameplay.Tags`, `.Attributes`, `.Effects`, `.Abilities`, `.Timers`, `.StateMachines`, `.Actions`, the possession properties of `.Player` and `.Pawn`, property 18 `FollowPawn` of `.CameraDirector`; resource `Zenith.Gameplay.RequestQueue` | own rows of what it writes and of the player and director configuration; any-row `Zenith.Gameplay.Owner` and `Zenith.Gameplay.Pawn`'s `Flags` (both `CommandsOnly`); the queue; resource `Zenith.Runtime.WorldState` | the GAME events of `Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME/Framework.md` → "Event types" | none (AI's phase-7 systems and `Gameplay.Rules` access disjoint data; hazards order any shared read) |
| `Gameplay.Cameras` | ZenithGameplay | 7 AI and navigation | `JOB_AFFINITY_ANY` | the state properties of `Zenith.Gameplay.CameraRig` and `Zenith.Gameplay.CameraDirector` | own rows of the rig and director components (the director's `FollowPawn` included) and of the output camera's `Zenith.Render.Camera`; any-row `Zenith.LocalTransform` and `Zenith.WorldTransform` (no phase-7 writer); resources `Zenith.TransformHierarchy` and `Zenith.CollisionQuery` | `SetProperty` commands on the output camera's `Zenith.LocalTransform` and `Zenith.Render.Camera` | `After("Gameplay.Rules")` |

<!-- elaboration -->
Phase 7 is the one phase whose inputs are every final pose of the tick (phase 6) and whose outputs, written as commands to the camera's local transform, are still propagated in the same tick (phase 8) and extracted in the same frame (phase 12). A camera computed in phase 10 would be propagated only in the next tick, adding one tick of camera latency to every frame; a camera computed in phase 6 could not read the targets' local transforms, which phase 6 writes ([Z2-N-ARCH-711]'s cross-entity rule as `Zenith2/Docs/Design/ECS/Scheduling.md` → "Authority and schedule checks" step 7 enforces it). The gameplay rules share phase 7 so their events are delivered in phase 9 of the same tick ([Z2-N-ARCH-478]) and late behaviours react in the tick that caused them. Candidate R6 of § "Refinement candidates" asks the ruling to name both nodes in phase 7's "Typical nodes" column.

- GAME's nodes do not fan out sub-jobs: ZenithGameplay's declared dependencies (`Zenith2/Docs/Architecture.md` → "Library dependency graph") do not include ZenithJobs, so no GAME source includes a ZenithJobs header or calls `Zenith_JobContext::ParallelFor` (libgraph's used-but-undeclared rule, [Z2-N-ARCH-167]). `Gameplay.Rules` and `Gameplay.Cameras` are single-node systems whose cost is linear in the requests and the gameplay rows of the world (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME/Framework.md` → "Rules algorithm").
- Neither GAME node declares a `ReadAnyRow` of a property any phase-7 node writes, so neither can raise `SCHEDULE_ERROR_CROSS_ENTITY_READ` ([Z2-N-ARCH-711]); each reads and writes its own-row-only columns only on the row of the entity it is processing, passing cross-entity facts through its node arena (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME/Framework.md` → "Row ownership in the rules node"; `Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME/Cameras.md` → "Declared access"). The possessed pawn reaches the camera director through the director's own property 18, which `Gameplay.Rules` writes, never through the player's `Pawn`.
- Neither GAME node writes a property whose authority is another phase; every write outside phase 7 (the camera's local transform, owned by phase 6; the render camera's properties, owned by phase 10) is a `SetProperty` command applied at sync point 7 ([Z2-N-ARCH-483], [Z2-N-ARCH-489]).

## Authority census

### Census format

The census is the D-PHASES ruling's evidence: the list of "every engine component field with its owning phase" its acceptance rule names. It lives in `Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/AuthorityCensus.md`, written first by the analysis ticket (§ "The D-PHASES ruling") and kept current by change-control rule 3. One Markdown table, one row per (component or resource, property group):

```text
| Area | Library | Stage | Stable name | Kind | Properties | Owning phase | Writer sources | Arbitration | Writers (system names) | Readers outside the owning phase | Source |
```

- `Library` is the CMake target that registers the type (`ZenithScene`, `ZenithRuntime`, `ZenithGameplay`, `ZenithPhysics`, …); the census tests select rows by it.
- `Stage` is `S0` … `S7`: the stage of the ticket that first registers the type, taken from the registering design's "Stage plan" by the analysis ticket and written as the current stage by every subsequent diff that adds a row (change-control rule 3). The census tests use it to tell a type that has not landed yet from one that is missing.
- `Kind` is `component`, `resource` or `event`; events carry no authority and appear with owning phase `—` so their emitters and readers are visible.
- `Properties` lists property IDs and names; a group of properties with one owning phase and one writer set is one row.
- `Owning phase` is a `Zenith_GetPhaseName` string (`EarlyScripts`, `CommandsOnly`, …).
- `Writer sources` lists the `Zenith_WriterSource` names of the arbitrated writers, or `—`.
- `Arbitration` is `none`, `Precedence (default order)`, `Precedence (<order>)`, `WeightedBlend` or `Custom (<function>)`.
- `Source` is the citation `Zenith2/Docs/Design/<CODE>.md` (or its split file) and heading that registers the type.

The census is generated by nothing: it is a reviewed document, and the file `Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/AuthorityCensus.md` is its only copy — the table of § "Census of signed-off designs" below is the analysis ticket's input, never read by a test. Three census tests, one per Lifecycle suite (§ "Suite target and fixtures"; `Zenith2/Docs/Design/GAME/Tests.md` → "Lifecycle per-command tests"), are the mechanism of change-control rule 3: `Test_Gameplay_AuthorityCensusMatchesRegistry` (Stage 2 suite; suite stage 2; linked: the Stage 2 list below), `Test_AuthorityCensus_CharacterModules` (Stage 3 suite; suite stage 3; + ZenithGameplay, ZenithPhysics, ZenithAnimation) and `Test_AuthorityCensus_OpenWorldModules` (Stage 4 suite; suite stage 4; + ZenithCinematics, ZenithStreaming, ZenithAI). Each reads the file at test time from `${ZENITH_SOURCE_DIR}/Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/AuthorityCensus.md` and the frozen type registry of a headless start of its suite's composition, and calls the shared comparison of the header-only `Zenith2/Engine/ZenithRuntime/Tests/Support/Zenith_AuthorityCensusCheck.h` (test support: headers only, compiled by no target, [Z2-N-ARCH-255]; tests may use the STL):

```cpp
// Zenith2/Engine/ZenithRuntime/Tests/Support/Zenith_AuthorityCensusCheck.h
struct Zenith_CensusCheckDesc
{
	const char* m_szCensusPath = nullptr;                   // the census file, read when m_strCensusText is empty
	std::string m_strCensusText;                            // a census given inline (the check's own SUBCASEs)
	std::vector<std::string> m_axLinkedLibraries;           // the suite's libraries, as the census `Library` cells spell them
	uint32 m_uSuiteStage = 0;                              // 2, 3 or 4
	uint32 m_uAcceptanceStage = 0;                         // 0 in the Test step; N in the Stage-N acceptance run, which sets the
	                                                        //   environment variable ZENITH_ACCEPTANCE_STAGE=N (rule 5)
};
struct Zenith_CensusCheckReport
{
	std::vector<std::string> m_axFailures;                  // the test fails when this is non-empty; each line names both sides
	std::vector<std::string> m_axSkipped;                   // printed to the test's output with MESSAGE
};
inline Zenith_CensusCheckReport Zenith_CheckAuthorityCensus(const Zenith_TypeRegistry& xRegistry, const Zenith_CensusCheckDesc& xDesc);
```

Each test sets `m_uAcceptanceStage` from the environment variable `ZENITH_ACCEPTANCE_STAGE` (an unsigned decimal; 0 when unset or unparsable, which the test prints). The linked-library lists are exact, spelled as the census `Library` cells and the CMake target names of `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture.md` → "Library dependency graph":

- **Stage 2** (`Test_Gameplay_AuthorityCensusMatchesRegistry`): ZenithRuntime and every library in its transitive declared dependencies — ZenithCore, ZenithGraph, ZenithProfiling, ZenithReflect, ZenithVertexFormat, ZenithCompression, ZenithDocument, ZenithJobs, ZenithPlatform, ZenithRHI, ZenithAssets, ZenithECS, ZenithScene, ZenithShadersGenerated, ZenithFlux, ZenithSave, ZenithScript, ZenithRuntime — plus the suite's three backends ZenithECS_Custom, ZenithECS_Flecs and ZenithECS_EnTT (21 names).
- **Stage 3** (`Test_AuthorityCensus_CharacterModules`): the Stage 2 list plus ZenithGameplay, ZenithPhysics, ZenithPhysics_Jolt and ZenithAnimation.
- **Stage 4** (`Test_AuthorityCensus_OpenWorldModules`): the Stage 3 list plus ZenithCinematics, ZenithStreaming and ZenithAI.

A library added to ZenithRuntime's declared dependencies joins the Stage 2 list in the diff that adds the edge.

The comparison, in this order (every rule is stage- and test-type-aware, so each test is green on the day its suite lands and stays green as subsequent stages add types):

1. **Parse.** Every table row of the file is read; a row whose `Stage` is not `S0` … `S7`, whose stable name is empty, or whose stable name begins `Zenith.Test.` fails (`malformed census row <n>: <reason>`): test types never have census rows.
2. **Test types are excluded.** Every registered type whose stable name begins `Zenith.Test.` (the probe module's `Zenith.Test.LifecycleProbe`, any test-local type) is ignored in both directions.
3. **Unlinked rows are skipped.** A row whose `Library` is not in the suite's list is listed `skipped: <stable name> (<library> not linked by this suite)`.
4. **Every registered property must match its row** (change-control rule 3's teeth). For each other registered component, resource and event type: its stable name must have a row whose `Library` is a linked library (else `registered type <stable name> has no census row`, or `… has a census row naming unlinked library <library>`); every registered property ID must appear in exactly one of that type's rows (else `registered property <stable name>.<id> <name> has no census row`, or `… appears in two rows`), and its registered owning phase and arbitration rule must equal the row's `Owning phase` and `Arbitration` cells (else `<stable name>.<id>: registered <phase>/<rule>, census <phase>/<rule>`; an event type carries no authority, so its row's `—` and `none` are what it is compared with); a row's property ID that the registered type lacks fails (`census row <stable name> names property <id>, which is not registered`).
5. **Rows of types not registered yet.** A row of a linked library whose stable name is not registered fails when its `Stage` is lower than the suite stage, or when `m_uAcceptanceStage` is not 0 and its `Stage` is at or below it (`census row <stable name> (Stage S<n>) has no registered type`: the type should have landed with that stage, whose acceptance the run checks); otherwise it is listed `skipped: <stable name> registers at S<n>`. The suite stages are fixed at 2, 3 and 4, so without the acceptance stage a renamed or withdrawn row of Stage S4–S7 would never be reported; every stage acceptance run from Stage 2 on (`Zenith2Acceptance`, Z2-D-083) runs the three census tests with `ZENITH_ACCEPTANCE_STAGE` set to its stage, which reports it at the latest in the acceptance of the row's own stage. So the Stage 2 test skips GAME's own `Zenith.Runtime.WorldState` and `Zenith.Runtime.CarryOver` rows (Stage S3, ZenithRuntime) until the S3 world-host ticket registers them, and compares them from then on, because the Stage 2 suite keeps running in every subsequent stage.

### Census rows added by subsequent designs

The areas whose designs follow GAME (PHYS, ANIM, AI, CINE, AUDIO, UI, VFX, TWOD, STREAM, NET, SAVE) add their component and resource rows, with `Stage` set to the stage of the registering ticket, in the diff that registers each type (change-control rule 3). The census test of the suite that links the registering library fails the first build in which it registers a property the census lacks, so a subsequent design cannot land a type without its census row:

| Registering library | Census test that compares its rows |
|---|---|
| the Stage 2 list of § "Census format" (ZenithRuntime and every library in its transitive declared dependencies, plus the three ECS backends) | `Test_Gameplay_AuthorityCensusMatchesRegistry` (Stage 2 suite) and, because every suite links the runtime, the Stage 3 and Stage 4 tests too |
| ZenithGameplay, ZenithPhysics (with ZenithPhysics_Jolt), ZenithAnimation | `Test_AuthorityCensus_CharacterModules` (Stage 3 suite) and `Test_AuthorityCensus_OpenWorldModules` |
| ZenithCinematics, ZenithStreaming, ZenithAI | `Test_AuthorityCensus_OpenWorldModules` (Stage 4 suite) |
| any other module (ZenithAudio, ZenithUI, ZenithVFX, Zenith2D, ZenithNet, ZenithEnvironment, …) | none of the three: no Lifecycle suite links it, so its rows are checked by review in the registering diff and by the D-PHASES ruling; the owning area's own registration-shape test asserts its authorities |

Designs that cite `Test_Gameplay_AuthorityCensusMatchesRegistry` as the evidence for a module's rows cite the test of the suite that links the module instead (cross-area row GA20). The D-PHASES ruling therefore rules on the rows that exist when it runs and on the rule that every subsequent row follows: a subsequent row that contradicts a ruled candidate (for example, a second unarbitrated phase-6 writer of `Zenith.LocalTransform`) is a new decision record under change-control rule 2.

## The D-PHASES ruling

### Analysis ticket

An ordinary (loop-run) Stage 1 ticket, "D-PHASES refinement analysis", writes two files and changes no code:

1. `Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/AuthorityCensus.md`: the census of § "Census of signed-off designs", one row per property group, every row with its source citation, in the format of § "Census format".
2. `Zenith2/Docs/Research/Phases/RefinementCandidates.md`: the table of § "Refinement candidates", each row extended with: the normative IDs it touches, every design heading that states an assumption about it (found by searching `Zenith2/Docs/Design/` for "D-PHASES" and for the assumption fences naming GAME), and a "Contradictions" cell listing any two designs that assume different outcomes (an empty cell reads `none found`).

The ticket is `kind: decision`'s preparatory half in the sense of Z2-D-084: it measures nothing, so no harness is built; its DoD is that both files exist with every candidate and every signed-off design's types covered.

### Ruling ticket

"Rule D-PHASES refinement" is the `needs-human` ruling TASK of Stage 1, blocked by the analysis ticket. The person:

1. Reads both research files and decides each candidate R1–R13: confirmed, or amended with the new text.
2. Appends to `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` one entry `### Z2-D-NNN D-PHASES refinement: …` with the next free ID (Z2-D-097's mechanism; the entry's title names the confirmed contract), whose body lists: every candidate's outcome; the census by reference (the file and its blob hash); the five scenario tests of § "Scenario suite" with their expected outcomes by reference; and every amendment's consequence per consumer area.
3. Applies each amendment in the same diff to `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/FramePhases.md` or `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/LifecycleCommands.md` with a new tagged bullet where an obligation changes (Z2-D-085), and to this file's § "Refinement candidates" (the "Default if confirmed" column becomes "Ruled").
4. Moves the D-PHASES block of `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` → "Open decisions" to settled (the open entry's status line reads "Ruled by Z2-D-NNN").

The acceptance rule is the D-PHASES entry's own (every engine component field with its owning phase, every contested field with its arbitration rule, every lifecycle publication point confirmed or amended, the five scenarios specified with expected outcomes); the census, the candidates file and § "Scenario suite" are its evidence.

## Phase contract budget rows

### Event queue capacity

Z2-B-GAME-003 "Event queue capacity per tick" is the value of ECS's configuration key `ecs.maxEventsPerTick`, the `ECS_CAPACITY_CLASS_EVENT_RECORDS` class of `Zenith2/Docs/Design/ECS/SystemAPI.md` → "Zenith_MergeContext header" (design default 65,536 while pending, `Zenith2/Docs/Design/ECS.md` → "Configuration keys"). GAME's ruling sets it; ECS enforces it at every emitting merge, first-fit in merge order (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/ECS/Scheduling.md` → "Capacity acceptance"); SCRIPT derives the behaviour event arrays (24 bytes per delivered event) and the retention ceiling from it (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/SCRIPT/Transactions.md` → "Retention ceiling derivation").

The Stage 1 per-system budget ticket for GAME derives the value by this rule from published and content-specification figures only: it measures nothing, the reference engine included, so the producer stays a single `needs-human` ruling ticket with no harness half, as `Zenith2/Docs/Budgets.md`'s producer table records it ([Z2-N-BUDGET-021]). Because the rule consumes three figures other tickets set, the ticket is `blocks`-linked from each of them in addition to the CORE, JOBS and RPROD rulings of [Z2-N-BUDGET-023]: "Stage 1 workload scale ticket for W-Dense" (E_peak's first term), "Stage 1 flagship acceptance ticket for OpenWorldSlice" (the content specification's simultaneous AI agents) and "Stage 1 workload scale ticket for W-Characters" (Z2-B-GAME-001's character count, § "Movement arbitration phase CPU"); the `Blocked by` cell of `Zenith2/Docs/Budgets.md` → "Producer tickets" gains the three in cross-doc item X254 (cross-area row GA8):

1. E_peak = the larger of: the W-Dense scale's per-tick emitting entities × 2 (the figure of "Stage 1 workload scale ticket for W-Dense"), and the OpenWorldSlice content specification's simultaneous AI agents × 4 (the content specification's figure; four gameplay events per agent per tick — a tag change, a timer, a state change and one message — is the ruling's stated assumption, recorded in its allocation table).
2. V = the smallest power of two ≥ 4 × E_peak, clamped to [16,384, 262,144].
3. The ruling checks the derived memory: SCRIPT's per-node delivered-event array is 24 × V bytes and the ECS event arena holds V records; both are reported in the allocation table, and V is reduced to the next lower power of two (never below 4 × E_peak) if the event arena alone would exceed 1% of Z2-B-ECS-001 "Component storage per world".

The row's tolerance is `0 (hard)` and its breach class `BR-MERGE`: exceeding it is a deterministic merge-time rejection of the emitting entity with a diagnostic, never a crash ([Z2-N-SCRIPT-333]).

### Movement arbitration phase CPU

Z2-B-GAME-001 "Movement arbitration phase CPU" is measured with MP-CPUZONE as the wall-clock span from the start of the first node of phase 4 to the completion of the last node of phase 4 of the primary world, per frame summed over its ticks, p95 over the measured interval (`Zenith2/Docs/Budgets.md` → "Per-system rows", bullet [Z2-N-BUDGET-221]). The nodes of phase 4 are those of candidate R13 (the orchestrator's ruling on question Q72): PHYS's `Physics.CharacterMove`, which applies ANIM's root-motion delta to controller entities, ANIM's `Animation.ApplyRootMotion` for entities without a controller, and ECS's arbitration nodes of the properties written in phase 4; GAME registers none.
