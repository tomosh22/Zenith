## Coverage

`Coverage.ps1 [-Stage <N>]` is the production successor of the session coverage checker (Z2-D-114): the same rules, in PowerShell, read-only against the committed board and documents (Z2-D-067). It is protected, it never writes a tracked file, and it never calls `zagent`.

### Inputs

| Input | Read from |
|---|---|
| the inventory | `Zenith2/Docs/Requirements/Inventory.md` → "Inventory v1" (one table per area) |
| the requirement catalogues | every `Zenith2/Docs/Requirements/<CODE>.md` and its split files `Zenith2/Docs/Requirements/<CODE>/*.md`: each `### Z2-R-<CODE>-NNN <title>` block with its eight labelled lines |
| the tickets | every `Zenith2/Docs/Board/tickets/Z2-*.md`: the Vault frontmatter (`key`, `type`, `title`, `category`, `parent`, `sprint`, `status`, `complexity`, `risk`, `storyPoints`, `labels`, `fixVersions`, `blocks`) and the body's managed region (the ten sections and the trailer lines `Requirements:`, `Decisions:`, `Open-Decisions:`, `Kind:`, `Draft-ID:`, `Spec-Maturity:`, `Cites:`, `Spec-Hash:`) |
| sprints and versions | `Zenith2/Docs/Board/sprints/*.md`, `Zenith2/Docs/Board/versions.md` |
| decisions | `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` headings `### Z2-D-NNN …` (settled and ruled) and `### D-…` (open) |
| budget rows | `Zenith2/Docs/Budgets.md` → "Row index" |
| the protected-path list | `vault.config.json` `policy.resolved.guardrails.protectedPaths` (the list `zagent guard` enforces) |
| the scanned documents | `Zenith2/Docs/Architecture.md`, `Rendering.md`, `Scripting.md`, `Contracts.md`, `Budgets.md` and their split directories |
| the tracked coverage files | `Zenith2/Docs/Coverage.md`, `Zenith2/Docs/Requirements/Normative.md` |

The ticket kind comes from the `Kind:` trailer line of the managed region (`implementation`, `api`, `decision`, `harness`, `budget`, `revalidation`, `integration`, `acceptance`, `proc`, `maintenance`; `Zenith2/Docs/Roadmap.md` → "Board conventions"); a non-epic ticket without one is the `fail` finding `CV-KIND-MISSING`. The production filer writes the trailer. For the seed, the session filer and the session checker's body module emit it, together with `Managed-Fields:`, in the production order of "The managed region". The orchestrator owns that change to the session tools (ruling PROC-R1, F11), and its check is a precondition of `Zenith2/Docs/Design/PROC/HumanProcedures.md` → "A6 dry run".

All under `${ZENITH_OUT}/coverage/`, that is `Zenith2/out/<checkout-hash>/coverage/` (gitignored), like every other gate output ([Z2-N-CONTRACT-573], [Z2-N-ARCH-160]; ruling PROC-R1 (7), reconciliation R10):

### Coverage.md format

The tracked `Zenith2/Docs/Coverage.md` is written by the filer (A8c, then reconcile runs) and regenerated by this step; the two must be byte-identical, so the format is fixed:

- Line 1 `# Zenith2 requirement coverage`, then a blank line, then the two explanatory paragraphs, each followed by a blank line, then `Tickets are shown as \`<key> (<Draft-ID>)\`.`, then `## Summary` with the table `| Area | Requirements | Required | Deferred | Research | Game-side | Excluded | Covered |`, one row per area that has requirements, in the order of `areaOrder`, and a final `| **All** | … |` row.
- The two paragraphs are data, not code: `Coverage/rules.json` member `coverageMdPreamble` (an array of two strings) holds them byte for byte as the session checker's `renderCoverageMd` writes them at the A8c commit. The orchestrator updates the session checker's text so that it names `Zenith2/out/<checkout-hash>/coverage/implemented.md` and cites no planning document before A8c (cross-area note to the orchestrator). `areaOrder` (an array of the 41 area codes) holds the session checker's `AREA_CODES` order: CORE, JOBS, PLAT, ECS, SCENE, STREAM, ASSET, PACK, RHI, GEOM, LIGHT, ENV, ANIM, PHYS, AI, GAME, SAVE, AUDIO, UI, VFX, CINE, EDIT, DEVT, SHIP, ADVGFX, NET, MOD, GRAPH, COMPRESS, SCRIPT, TWOD, CONTENT, WORLD, RPROD, CHAR, ONLINE, SEC, MAINT, RESEARCH, SCOPE, PROC. A new area is appended to both lists by a PROC ticket.
- Then per area `## <CODE>`, and per stage present `### <CODE> stage <S>` (the `Stage:` value verbatim, `S0` … `S7`) with the table `| Requirement | Title | Classification | Leaf tickets | Proof tickets |`, rows byte-ordered by requirement ID.
- A ticket cell is `—` when empty, otherwise the comma-and-space-joined, byte-ordered list of `<key> (<Draft-ID>)`; a title has `|` escaped as `\|` and runs of whitespace collapsed to one space.
- The leaf and proof columns follow the classification, as data: `Coverage/rules.json` members `leafKinds` and `proofKinds` (objects keyed by classification) hold the session checker's column rules as a recorded table.
  - required and deferred: leaf kinds `implementation`, `api`, `harness`, `proc` and `maintenance`, plus `budget` when the requirement's evidence is a budget-row measurement (ruling BUDGET-KIND-LEAF); proof kinds `integration` and `acceptance`;
  - research: leaf `harness` or `implementation`, proof `decision`;
  - game-side: leaf `implementation`, proof `integration` or `acceptance`;
  - excluded: no ticket.

  `Test_Coverage_SessionCheckerParity` compares both members with a recorded copy of the session checker's `IMPLEMENTATION_KINDS`, `INTEGRATION_KINDS` and column rules, kept in the fixture `Coverage/Fixtures/SessionCheckerKinds/kinds.json` (the production step cannot read the session checker). A `proc` or `maintenance` ticket therefore stays in the leaf column, and the regenerated `Coverage.md` equals the tracked one.
- LF line endings, a final newline, UTF-8 without BOM.

## Coverage rules

`Coverage/rules.json` lists every rule with its ID, its severity, the plan clause it implements, and the session-checker rule it corresponds to, so the production step and the session checker agree by construction:

| Rule | Severity | Check | Session-checker rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| CV-INVENTORY-UNMAPPED | fail | an inventory item that no requirement's `Covers:` names | `inventory-unmapped` |
| CV-REQ-NO-LEAF | fail | a required or deferred requirement without a leaf ticket of a leaf kind | `requirement-no-leaf` |
| CV-REQ-NO-PROOF | fail | a required, deferred, research or game-side requirement without a proof ticket | `requirement-no-integration` |
| CV-EPIC-ONLY | fail | a requirement whose only ticket is an EPIC | `epic-only-coverage` |
| CV-EXCLUDED | fail | an excluded requirement without a rationale and a `Z2-D-` ID in its `Decisions:` line, or with tickets | `excluded-no-rationale`, `excluded-has-tickets` |
| CV-SECTION | fail | a non-epic ticket missing one of the ten template sections, out of order, with a `##`/`###` heading inside Goal or DoD, or with a heading named `Gates` anywhere (Z2-D-099) | `section-missing`, `section-order`, `heading-inside-goal-dod`, `gates-heading` |
| CV-DOD-NO-FAILURE | fail | a Definition of Done without a failure scenario: no `- [ ]` item containing one of the failure terms of `rules.json`'s `failureTerms` list (`fails`, `refuses`, `rejects`, `error`, `exit`, `missing`, `corrupt`, `invalid`, `timeout`, `rolls back`, `is reported`, `asserts`) | `dod-no-failure` |
| CV-REF-UNPLANNED | fail | a referenced decision (`Decisions:`, `Open-Decisions:`), fixture or pending budget row that is neither existing nor planned; planned means a producer ticket exists and `blocks` the referencing ticket | `decision-unknown`, `budget-pending-no-producer`, `fixture-not-blocked`, `open-decision-no-producer` |
| CV-PENDING-NOT-BLOCKED | fail | a pending budget or open decision reference whose producer does not `block` the referencing ticket | `budget-pending-not-blocked`, `open-decision-not-blocked` |
| CV-ROUTING | fail | a non-epic ticket missing its category, parent epic, sprint, fix version, complexity, risk or points; an epic with a sprint or fix version; a STORY (Z2-D-109) | `board-epic-routing`, `board-sprint`, `board-fix-version`, `type-story` |
| CV-ACCEPTANCE | fail | a stage without exactly one acceptance ticket (kind `acceptance`, category `Zenith2Acceptance`, label `needs-human`, the sprint's gate); an acceptance ticket whose `Requirements:` line lacks a requirement of its stage or names one of another stage | `acceptance-missing`, `acceptance-incomplete`, `acceptance-foreign-requirement`, `acceptance-category`, `acceptance-needs-human`, `sprint-gate` |
| CV-LINK | fail | a `blocks` entry naming no ticket, a `blocks` edge without a reason, a cross-project link (Z2-D-112) | `blocked-by-dangling`, `blocked-by-reason`, `board-cross-project-link` |
| CV-CYCLE | fail | a cycle in the `blocks` graph (Tarjan's strongly connected components over every Z2 ticket, O(V + E)) | `blocks-cycle` |
| CV-DRAFT-ID | fail | a duplicate `Draft-ID:` across ticket files, or a non-epic ticket without one | `draft-id-duplicate` |
| CV-PROTECTED-WORK | fail | a ticket not labelled `needs-human` whose Definition of Done or Implementation Contract names a path matching a protected glob, after normalising `\` to `/` (`Zenith2/Docs/Roadmap.md` → "Board conventions", protected work is explicit) | `protected-path-dod`, `protected-path-contract` |
| CV-NEEDS-GPU | fail | a `needs-gpu` ticket without exactly one `gpu-reason:*` label | `needs-gpu-reason` |
| CV-GREP-GUARD | fail | a ticket body or a scanned document containing a Vault grep-guard pattern (Z2-D-100) | `grep-guard` |
| CV-HUMAN-PHRASE | fail | a ticket not labelled `needs-human` whose body contains a human-only phrase of [Z2-N-CONTRACT-513] | `human-only-phrase` |
| CV-DRIFT | fail | the regenerated `Coverage.md` differs from the tracked one; the finding prints the first differing line of each | — |
| CV-NORMATIVE-GRAMMAR | fail | in the five scanned documents, a keyword of `rules.json`'s `normativeKeywords` (`must`, `must not`, `required`, `never`, `forbidden`, `cannot`, `only`, `always`, `shall`) in prose outside a tagged bullet and outside an `<!-- informative -->` block; fenced code, tables and headings excluded (Z2-D-066) | `keyword-outside-tag` |
| CV-NORMATIVE-ID | fail | a duplicate `[Z2-N-<DOC>-NNN]` within a document family, or a tag whose `<DOC>` is not its family's | `tag-duplicate`, `tag-family` |
| CV-NORMATIVE-DRIFT | fail | the regenerated `Normative.md` differs from the tracked one | — |
| CV-STALE-CITE | report | a cite whose section or normative-statement hash changed since filing (reported, never failed: a living-docs ticket that edits its section passes) | `cite-heading`, `body-hash` |
| CV-CITE-UNRESOLVED | report | a cite whose path or heading no longer exists | `cite-unresolved` |
| CV-KIND-MISSING | fail | a non-epic ticket without a `Kind:` trailer | — |
| CV-SPEC-HASH | report | a managed region whose recomputed hash differs from its `Spec-Hash:` line (a hand edit since filing; reconcile refuses such a ticket) | `spec-hash` |

The cite hash algorithm is the session filer's, restated so the production tools reproduce it: the hash of a `(path, heading)` cite is the SHA-256 of the cited heading line through the line before the next heading of the same or higher level, fence-aware (a `#` line inside a fenced block is not a heading), CRLF normalised to LF, trailing blank lines trimmed; the hash of a normative ID is the SHA-256 of that tagged bullet's lines (the bullet line and its indented continuations).

## Coverage fixtures

`Zenith2/Tools/Gates/Tests/Coverage/Fixtures/<Rule>/` holds one fixture board per failure mode (`Zenith2/Docs/Roadmap.md` → "Stage 0 deliverables" item 9). Each fixture is a minimal tree: `Zenith2/Docs/Requirements/Inventory.md` with one area, its catalogue, `Zenith2/Docs/Board/{tickets,sprints}/` with two to six ticket files, `versions.md`, a `DecisionLog.md` and `Budgets.md` holding the rows the fixture needs, a tracked `Coverage.md` generated from the fixture itself (except in `CV-DRIFT`), and `expect.json`. The fixtures: one per rule of the table above (25), plus `Pass-Clean` (a fully covered one-area board), `Pass-PlannedReference` (a pending budget row whose producer blocks the referencing ticket), `Pass-StaleCiteReported` (exit 0 with a `CV-STALE-CITE` report), `Accept-MissingFixture` (acceptance mode `-Stage 1` with a planned but absent fixture: fail) and `Accept-Clean`. The self-test asserts `Test_Coverage_EachFixtureDetected` (the rule set of every fixture), `Test_Coverage_RenderMatchesTracked` (on `Pass-Clean`, regenerated `Coverage.md` equals the tracked file byte for byte), `Test_Coverage_NeverWritesTracked` (the fixture tree's tracked files are unchanged after a run), and `Test_Coverage_SessionCheckerParity` (every rule ID of `rules.json` names a session-checker rule or `—`, and every session-checker rule of the table is named once).

## Coverage acceptance mode

`Coverage.ps1 -Stage N` is run by the stage-N acceptance ticket through `Run-Committed.ps1 -Acceptance -Stage N`. On top of the default checks:

1. **Existence instead of planning.** For every ticket whose fix version is `Z2 Stage k` with `k ≤ N`: every referenced decision exists as a `### Z2-D-NNN` heading in `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md`; every referenced fixture exists on disk at the path the ticket names; every referenced budget row has status `adopted` in `Zenith2/Docs/Budgets.md` → "Row index". A planned-only reference is the `fail` finding `CV-ACCEPT-REF-MISSING`.
2. **Implemented coverage.** `implemented.md` is regenerated, and every requirement whose `Stage:` is `Sk` with `k ≤ N` must have every leaf and proof ticket Done (`status: Done`); otherwise `CV-ACCEPT-NOT-DONE`. Two exemptions apply (ruling PROC-R2 M1):
   - the stage-N acceptance ticket itself, the one ticket of kind `acceptance` whose fix version is `Z2 Stage N`, because the run precedes its finish;
   - every Draft-ID in the `coverageDeferred` array of stage `N` in `acceptance_suites.json` (the stage's complexity-profile apply ticket, whose ruling measures the accepted tree). The next stage's cumulative run requires it Done.

   No requirement may be attached to a ticket whose stage comes after its own (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/PROC/Acceptance.md` → "Acceptance ticket shape"), so no other exemption is needed. The finding `CV-ACCEPT-STAGE-ORDER` fails a requirement listed by a ticket whose fix version comes after its `Stage:`.
3. **Evidence validity.** For every evidence record a stage-≤N Done ticket attaches (the `.json` files its work log names under `Zenith2/Docs/Research/` or the acceptance run directory), `Test-Zenith2EvidenceRecord` (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/DEVT/Evidence.md` → "Zenith2Evidence module") must return `valid`; a mismatched fingerprint is `CV-ACCEPT-EVIDENCE-STALE` (Z2-D-065). The stage's own campaign record is read too, because the acceptance procedure runs this step after the campaign. For `N ≥ 1`, the tracked copies of stage `N-1` (`Zenith2/Docs/Research/Acceptance/stage-<N-1>/run.json`, `baseline-run.json`, `campaign-summary.json` from Stage 2, and `record.json`) must exist, and their `headSha` must be an ancestor of `HEAD`; otherwise `CV-ACCEPT-RECORD-MISSING`.
4. **Budget breaches.** An open BUG labelled with a BR-ACCEPT breach against a row the stage measures is `CV-ACCEPT-BREACH` ([Z2-N-CONTRACT-508]).
5. **Research rulings** (RESEARCH's P-RS2). Every requirement of stage `k ≤ N` whose classification is `research` must have a ruling entry in `Zenith2/Docs/DecisionLog.md` carrying a `**Research:**` field; a missing one is `CV-ACCEPT-RESEARCH-UNRULED`. The step also runs `Invoke-ResearchCheck.ps1 -Mode decisionlog` (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/RESEARCH/Tooling.md`) when it exists, and a failure is `CV-ACCEPT-RESEARCH-CHECK`; from Stage 1 an absent script is a `fail` with the same ID.

The acceptance-mode fixtures are `Accept-MissingFixture`, `Accept-NotDone`, `Accept-StaleEvidence` (a record whose fingerprint field differs from the recomputed one), `Accept-ResearchUnruled`, `Accept-OwnTicketInProgress` (the accepted ticket In Progress: passes), `Accept-DeferredApply` (the stage's apply ticket In Progress and listed in `coverageDeferred`: passes), `Accept-StageOrder` (fails), `Accept-PreviousRecordMissing` (fails) and `Accept-Clean`.

## Production filer

`Zenith2/Tools/Backlog/File-Backlog.ps1` is the production filer (Z2-D-068, Z2-D-069). It is not a gate step and not protected; it is a Z2 tool, run by humans and by the `postFinish` command. It talks to the board through `pwsh -NoProfile -File Tools/zagent/zagent.ps1 … --json` only, passing every argument as its own argv element.

### Command line

```
pwsh -NoProfile -File Zenith2/Tools/Backlog/File-Backlog.ps1 --file <drafts dir> --checked <coverage report> [--dry-run] [--limit N]
pwsh -NoProfile -File Zenith2/Tools/Backlog/File-Backlog.ps1 --reconcile <drafts dir> [--force-spec <KEY>] [--dry-run]
pwsh -NoProfile -File Zenith2/Tools/Backlog/File-Backlog.ps1 --mark-stale [--dry-run]
pwsh -NoProfile -File Zenith2/Tools/Backlog/File-Backlog.ps1 --write-coverage
```

The script parses these GNU-style arguments itself from `$args` (no PowerShell `param` block), because `postFinish` passes `--mark-stale` exactly as `Zenith2/Docs/Roadmap.md` spells it; an argument that starts with a single `-` is exit 2. Exit codes: 0 done; 1 stopped after a failed `zagent` call, with the resume instruction printed; 2 refused (a precondition failed; nothing was written).

### Pass 1 and pass 2

The filing passes are the session filer's (`Zenith2/Docs/Roadmap.md` → "Board conventions"; Z2-D-113): sprints first, epics, then leaves in Kahn topological order over `blocked_by`, with `--blocked-by … --reason` for the most common reason and `link … blocked-by … --reason` for the rest, `--sprint`, `--fix-version`, `--parent`, `--label` at create time, bodies through `create --file <absolute path>` after a `Test-Path` and verified by the SHA-256 of `show --json`'s `body` (Z2-D-111); pass 2 runs `sprint gate` per stage. Every write names `--project Z2` (Z2-D-090). The filer is keyed on Draft-ID: a re-run scans `Zenith2/Docs/Board/tickets/Z2-*.md` for `Draft-ID:` lines and skips existing tickets.

### The managed region

The filer owns, in every ticket body, exactly the managed region: the ten template sections and the trailer lines, ending with `Spec-Hash: <SHA-256 of every byte of the region before this line>`. Everything else is preserved byte for byte in its original position: the `## Comments` section, the work-log block between `<!-- agent:worklog:start <KEY> -->` and `<!-- agent:worklog:end -->`, every review-record block between `<!-- agent:review:start <KEY> -->` and `<!-- agent:review:end -->`, and any other unmanaged content. The trailer lines, in order: `Requirements:`, `Decisions:`, `Open-Decisions:` (when non-empty), `Kind:`, `Managed-Fields:`, `Draft-ID:`, `Spec-Maturity:`, `Cites:` with one `- …` line per cite, `Spec-Hash:`. `Managed-Fields:` lists the frontmatter fields the filer set at filing, separated by `; ` (`labels=needs-human,deferred; points=3; sprint=z2-stage-1-architecture-validation; fixVersion=Z2 Stage 1; parent=Z2-41`), which is how reconcile changes only fields it set originally.

The trailer order is fixed for the production filer and for the session filer that files the seed:

```
Requirements: <IDs, comma-space separated, or none>
Decisions: <IDs or none>
Open-Decisions: <names>            (only when non-empty)
Kind: <kind>                       (every non-epic ticket; from the draft's kind)
Managed-Fields: <field=value; …>   (the fields set at create: labels, points, sprint, fixVersion, parent)
Draft-ID: <Draft-ID>
Spec-Maturity: ready|draft
Cites:
- <path> → "<heading>" <sha256>
Spec-Hash: <sha256>
```

A ticket with no `Managed-Fields:` line (a ticket filed by hand, or one filed before this rule) is reconciled body-only: its managed region is rewritten and none of its frontmatter is touched.

### Reconcile algorithm

For each draft whose Draft-ID exists on the board (`--reconcile`):

1. `zagent show <KEY> --project Z2 --json`; split the body into the unmanaged prefix (none), the managed region (from `## Goal` through the `Spec-Hash:` line) and the unmanaged suffix (everything after).
2. Recompute the hash of the current managed region. If it differs from the region's own `Spec-Hash:` line (someone edited the spec since filing), refuse this ticket with a unified diff of the current region against the region as filed, unless `--force-spec <KEY>` names exactly this ticket.
3. Generate the new managed region from the draft (same generator as filing), with a fresh `Spec-Hash:`.
4. When the new region equals the current one byte for byte, do nothing for the body.
5. Otherwise build the new body as `<new managed region>` + `<the unmanaged suffix, unchanged>` and write it with `zagent update <KEY> --body <temp file>` (`--body` replaces the whole body after the frontmatter, so the suffix must be re-attached exactly).
6. Metadata: for each field in `Managed-Fields:` whose draft value differs: labels through `update --label`/`--unlabel` (only labels the filer set), points through `update --points`, sprint through `sprint remove` then `sprint add`, fix version through `version set`, parent through `parent`; never a field the filer did not set.
7. Links: list `zagent links <KEY> --json`; add each draft `blocked_by` edge missing on the board with `link … --reason "backlog: …"`; remove with `unlink` each board edge whose reason starts `backlog:` and that the draft no longer carries; never touch an edge whose reason does not start `backlog:`.
8. Revalidation labels: when the draft's `Spec-Maturity` is `ready` and the ticket carries `needs-revalidation`, remove `needs-revalidation` and `deferred` (Z2-D-102).
9. Record the step in `${ZENITH_OUT}/backlog/reconcile-<UTC>.jsonl`, one line per ticket, so an interrupted run's re-run converges: every step above is idempotent, because each compares before it writes.

`--dry-run` prints the planned writes of every step and writes nothing.

### --mark-stale

Run by the Z2 `postFinish` command after every Z2 ticket finishes Done (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/PROC/Harness.md` → "postFinish"):

1. Read every Z2 ticket's `Cites:` lines; recompute each cite's hash from the documents at the tip of `master` (`git show master:<path>`, so the result does not depend on the working tree of whichever checkout runs it).
2. For each ticket in To Do with at least one changed hash and without `needs-revalidation`: `zagent update <KEY> --label needs-revalidation,deferred`, then set `Spec-Maturity: draft` inside the managed region through the reconcile write path (the region changes, so its `Spec-Hash:` is recomputed and the change is not a hand edit).
3. Skip tickets In Progress, In Review, Done or Blocked.
4. Print one line per marked ticket and exit 0; a failed `zagent` call is exit 1 and leaves the tickets already marked marked (the next run converges).

### --check-headroom

Run by the Z2 `postFinish` command after every Z2 ticket finishes Done (ruling PROC-R2 M9). It implements [Z2-N-BUDGET-353]'s rule that three consecutive tickets above 80 % of a warm phase cap trigger a MAINT headroom ticket, with no `TICK.md` edit:

1. Find the finished ticket's `tick_gates.json` under `.zagent/run/<KEY>/` in the main checkout, or in the registered lane that ran it (`zagent lane list --json` paths); a ticket gated by hand has none, and the option exits 0 with `headroom: no gate record for <KEY>`.
2. Append one line to `.zagent/headroom/Z2.jsonl` (gitignored scratch in the main checkout): `{ key, finishedUtc, measureSeconds, verifySeconds, measureCold, verifyCold }`. Each phase's seconds is the sum of its `steps[].Seconds`; the cold flags come from `cold.applied`.

Exception: when an open ticket of that title prefix exists, it prints `headroom: <title> already open` instead. The body's evidence is the three lines and their `Test` per-preset figures.
5. Exit 0 in every case except a failed `zagent` call (exit 1). The mid-sprint check stays the backstop.

## ApkCheck

`ApkCheck.ps1`, active in the base category from Stage 2 (Z2T-PROC-040). It checks the 16 KB page compliance of every Android binary and APK and the dex contents of every game APK (`Zenith2/Docs/Architecture/BuildSystem.md` → "Android packaging", the build-type table).

### Inputs and builds

1. Ensure `android-arm64` (Release) is built.
2. Build `zenith_apks` in the `android-arm64` tree (`cmake --build --preset android-arm64 --target zenith_apks`), which runs each game's `<Name>_Apk` (`Zenith2/Docs/Design/MOD/GameBuild.md` → "The Apk target"; MOD-P3, row M20).

### Checks

| Check | Command | Finding |

### Smoke selection and budget

With ticket context, `Sanitizers -Preset win-asan` runs the 60-second fuzz smokes selected by `Zenith2/Docs/Design/SEC/Fuzzing.md` → "Smoke selection" (SEC-P2): the step builds the selected `Zenith_Fuzz_<Stem>.Smoke` list from the changed set, then passes the other smokes to `ctest -E`.

- Smokes run with `--parallel J`, where `J = min(8, floor(logical processors / 2))`.
- When `ceil(N / J) × 60 s` exceeds 960 s for `N` selected smokes, each smoke runs `max(15, floor(960 × J / N))` seconds instead, through the fuzz main's `-max_total_time` argument, and the step logs `Sanitizers: fuzz smoke shortened to <s> s for <N> targets` (SEC's message).
