## Goal
GDD §9.2 to §9.4 (`MVPScope.md` §2.1 defers all three): Daily Vexholme (one shared seed per calendar day from the shuffler, one attempt, a local score), Endless Vexholme (Nights continue past 7 with the difficulty curve extrapolated and the shuffler cycling content), Hauntings (a curated Night variant list as data). All three are front-end entries over the shuffler (DP-41) and the meta-save; no networking (Scope: single-player only), so the daily seed is derived from the date locally and the score is a local best. Tests: the daily seed is stable within a day and changes across days; Endless advances past Night 7 and stays deterministic; a haunting applies its variant row.
