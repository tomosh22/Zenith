## Goal
MVPScope §2.2: *"Procedural shuffler (reagent anchors, locked doors, villager set, weather)"* and §2.1: *"6 of 7 Nights of the campaign"*; GDD §5.5 (Night-to-Night variance) and §9.1 (campaign). The procgen already generates the layout from a seed; the shuffler layers a per-Night draw on top: which 5 of 14 reagents (DP-34), their anchors, which doors are locked, the villager set from the 24 (DP-32/33), the Aelfric variant (DP-36), hounds from Night 3 (DP-35), escalation schedule (DP-37) and a weather value that feeds fog density. A 7-Night campaign sequences the draws with GDD §5.3's difficulty curve, carried between Nights by `DP_MetaSave` (which fields persist per Night is a schema bump with a migration test per `Docs/SaveFormat.md`). Integer-deterministic from the campaign seed so `Test_ProcLevel_DeterminismCheck` extends to the shuffle. Tests: the shuffle is deterministic and covers every content row over the campaign, the campaign advances on a win and resets per the GDD on a loss, and an automated 7-Night campaign test plays a scripted win per Night.
