## Goal
MVPScope §2.2: *"Meta-progression (hermit unlock trees)"*; GDD §5.4. Knots (`DP_Knots`, banked per run) buy permanent unlocks on hermit trees: new distractions and charms into the shuffle pool, a starting-reagent hint, a longer possession range, an extra hand-off chain link. Data-driven tree in `Config/` (nodes, costs from the tuned curve DP-30, prerequisites), persisted in `DP_MetaSave` (schema bump with a migration test per `Docs/MetaSaveFormat.md`), applied at Night start by the shuffler and tuning. Tests: purchase, prerequisite refusal, persistence across a reload, and each unlock's effect observed in a Night; the matrix re-run at zero unlocks (the criteria hold with nothing bought) and at all unlocks (upper bound recorded).

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A data-driven unlock tree persists through the meta-save and applies at Night start, with purchase, prerequisite and effect tests
- [ ] Matrix criteria hold at zero unlocks; the all-unlocks upper bound is recorded
- [ ] `MVPScope.md` §2.2 records meta-progression shipped
