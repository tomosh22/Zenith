## Goal
TDD §12.3 and §12.6. `Source/Render/UV_TileInstances`: one `Flux_InstanceGroup` per tile material, one instance per solid cell, drawn by the UnifiedMesh GPU-cull and indirect path into the G-buffer; a stable cell-to-(group, instance) table built at load; instances toggled with `SetInstanceEnabled`, NEVER `RemoveInstance` (removal is swap-and-pop and shifts ids); `SetInstanceColor` as the overlay and selection tint lane. The procedural tile kit (§12.6): tile meshes and materials per biome recipe generated and baked by the tools boot, deterministic from seeds, byte-identical on re-bake, committed under `Assets/` per the manifest discipline. Pure halves (record packing, chunk cull rects) in their own headers with headless units; a windowed capture of the Standard map from the ortho camera. `needs-gpu`: the bake and the visual check need a device.

- [ ] A windowed capture of the Standard map is attached; the per-frame upload size is recorded against §12.5
