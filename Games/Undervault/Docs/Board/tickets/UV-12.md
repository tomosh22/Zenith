## Goal
TDD §20 Phase 0 ④: *"Fluid-feel prototype on Windows (tune flow constants at 5 Hz)."* This is the first real simulation code and the Vertical Slice reuses it verbatim. Implement `Source/Grid/UV_CellGrid` (§3.1 SoA arrays, double-buffered element/mass/temperature, flags, occupancy; chunks as metadata §3.2) and `Source/Sim/UV_FluidSim` (§4.1: gather flux over right and up faces, `(m_a - m_b)/8` clamped, gas into vacuum `m_a/4`, two parity-coloured swap passes for displacement and density ordering, lateral liquid levelling, per-cell mass cap ~1,000 kg) on a `UV_SimClock` at 5 Hz, with a tools-only debug draw of the grid (coloured quads through `Flux_Quads`) so the constants can be tuned by eye on a small hand-built map (water pooling, CO2 pit, hydrogen ceiling). Units from §16.2 land WITH the code: exact per-tick mass conservation (double-precision sums), flux antisymmetry, equilibrium convergence, no flow across solid or vacuum faces, and worker-count invariance of the digest at 0, 1 and 7 workers. The tuned constants become the shipped defaults with the tuning recorded in `Docs/DecisionLog.md`.
