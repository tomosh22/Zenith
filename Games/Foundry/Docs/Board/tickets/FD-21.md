## Goal
TDD §7: SoA archetype tables (parallel arrays per field over `FD_SlotMap` with per-archetype freelists), no virtual dispatch, per-archetype tick functions. Crafting math in integers: progress accumulates `speed × satisfaction` (Q16.16) per active tick against a per-recipe target; ingredients debit on craft start, products credit on completion, transactional against output-full. Machines sleep when input-starved / output-blocked / unpowered-below-threshold; producers wake consumers through explicit wake links (belt→machine, machine→inserter). "No wake storms": satisfaction changes never traverse sleeper lists. Ship the generic `FD_ArchetypeTable<Fields...>` scaffolding and the **furnace** as the first archetype (stone furnace, coal fuel, ore→plate) with a minimal 3-item/2-recipe content stub that M1 replaces with real tables. Satisfaction is a constant 1.0 until M1's electric networks.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A furnace fed by inserter completes crafts at exactly the per-tick rate implied by the recipe (tick-exact completion pinned)
- [ ] A starved furnace sleeps: `uMachinesTicked` is 0 for it until the wake link fires (op-count pinned)
- [ ] Output-full blocks completion transactionally: ingredients are not lost and the craft completes on the tick space frees
- [ ] Fuel exhaustion is a timer-wheel event, not a per-tick poll
