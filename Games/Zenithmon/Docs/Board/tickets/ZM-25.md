## Goal
`Status.md` R1 slice table, **R1-9**: camera-clearance coverage for Route 1 and Thornacre, with the budget re-derived rather than carried over. This is critic blocker #4 from the R1 slice brief (`Status.md` "FIVE BLOCKERS THE CRITICS FOUND"), the only one still OPEN: the three camera-clearance tests in `Tests/ZM_AutoTests_CameraClearance.cpp` are HARD-SCOPED to Dawnmere (`iCC_DAWNMERE_BUILD_INDEX = 2`, `LoadSceneByIndex` at three sites), so Route 1 rows would raycast Route 1 columns against the DAWNMERE physics world and mostly fall off it. The constants also live in an anonymous namespace inside `#ifdef ZENITH_INPUT_SIMULATOR`, so no boot unit can name them.

**The fix the brief prescribes:** extract the sample enum, spacings and budget into a simulator-independent pure header under `Source/World/` (so boot units can see them), then parameterise the tests per region: one automated registration per region (Dawnmere stays; Route 1 and Thornacre are new), each loading its own committed scene against its own baked terrain on the Null backend (`m_bRequiresGraphics = false`, like the two existing registrations described in `TestPlan.md` §8).

**Adopted default from ZM-D-196:** coarsen the long-route spine spacing while KEEPING the 4x finer gate-approach spacing, and raise `uCC_MAX_SAMPLES`. At 1.0 m the Route 1 spine alone would overflow the 1024 ceiling (already 603 occupied by Dawnmere), and overflow is a hard FAILURE, never truncation. Re-derive the 50% arm-retention budget from fresh captures per region rather than copying Dawnmere's 6.0008 m figure; the sample tables are the enforceable boundary and deliberately carry no rings around NPCs (`TestPlan.md` §8, "the enforceable boundary, stated so it cannot be oversold").

Sequencing: R1-6 (ZM-24) adds two trainer bodies to Route 1's physics world first, which is why this ticket is blocked by it. R1-10 (ZM-26) adds the Warden afterwards and re-derives once more; ZM-72 ruling 4 records that order.
