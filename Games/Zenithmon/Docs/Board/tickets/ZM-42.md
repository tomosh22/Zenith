## Goal
`Roadmap.md` S11: *"Balance pass driven by headless AI-vs-AI simulation stats"*. The dex's base stats and learnsets are *"systematic placeholders for the S11 balance pass"* (ZM-D-021/023, Shortfalls §1.1), GDD §8's bands are *"tuning targets, not promises; the S11 balance pass owns the final numbers"*, and the gate is *"headless 100-streak simulation invariants"* over the tower (ZM-41).

Scope:
- **The simulator:** a headless tool-boot mode (`--balance-sim`) that runs N seeded AI-vs-AI battles through the real `ZM_BattleEngine` and `ZM_BattleAI` at chosen tiers, over (a) every species pair at matched level for a type-matchup matrix, (b) each gym leader's team against a band-typical player team built from that route's encounter tables, (c) 100-streak tower runs, and writes a report artefact under `Build/artifacts/` with win rates, mean turns, faint distributions and the slowest species. Deterministic: same seeds, same report, pinned by a digest unit on a tiny configuration.
- **The pass:** adjust base stats, learnsets and leader teams in `Source/Data/` until the report meets targets the GDD states or this ticket records (e.g. no species with a win rate under 30% or over 70% at matched level against its own tier; every leader between 55% and 75% against the band team; no battle mean over 40 turns; tower streaks ending inside a stated distribution). Every changed number is a data edit under the existing golden and integrity units; goldens that pin placeholder numbers are re-recorded with the reason in the commit.
- **Proof:** the 100-streak invariants (TestPlan §5.10) as units; the digest unit on the simulator; the targets asserted by a unit that reads the committed report artefact so a later data edit that breaks balance reds.

ZM-109 (battle fidelity deferrals) BLOCKS this: measuring balance against mechanics that then change wastes the pass.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] The balance simulator produces a deterministic report over the species matrix, every leader and the tower, pinned by a digest unit
- [ ] Base stats, learnsets and leader teams are tuned to the recorded targets; the targets are asserted by a unit over the committed report
- [ ] Headless 100-streak tower invariants hold
- [ ] GDD §8 and Shortfalls §1.1 drop the "placeholder" notes and record the final bands in the same commit
- [ ] Every affected game's pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED run
