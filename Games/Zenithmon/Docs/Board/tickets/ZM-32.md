## Goal
`Roadmap.md` S9: *"Encounter tables per route"*, for Routes 2-8. Fill each S9 route row's `ZM_EncounterSlot` array (`m_eSpecies`, `m_uMinLevel`, `m_uMaxLevel`, `m_uWeight`) and its per-route rate constant in `Source/Data/ZM_WorldSpec.cpp`, from three sources: GDD §2.2's encounter-field character per route (Route 4 leans aquatic near the surf, Route 6 Electric-heavy, Route 7 the safe egg-hatching circuit, Route 8 narrow wind-flattened shelves), GDD §8's wild bands (Routes 1-2 L2-8, Routes 2-3 L8-14, Routes 3-5 L13-19, Routes 5-7 L18-24, Routes 7-8 L23-29), and GDD §5's rarity tiers as the weight scale (COMMON/UNCOMMON/RARE; LEGENDARY never appears in a route table).

Route 1 is the precedent (ZM-22, ZM-D-204): its rate lives in `uZM_ROUTE1_ENCOUNTER_RATE`, the shared `uZM_DEFAULT_ROUTE_ENCOUNTER_RATE` is untouched, and `WorldSpec_EncounterRateColumn` only asserts `> 0`, so a per-route unit pins each spelled value. `Tests/ZM_Tests_Encounter.cpp` and `ZM_Tests_RouteEncounterSeam.cpp` drive the roll seam from a synthetic density map without a bake; extend them so every S9 route table is rolled through `RollStepForScene` and every slot is reachable (an anti-vacuity arm that a weight of zero or an inverted level band reds). Do not disturb the frozen RNG draw order (`RollStep_InertAndMissDoNotPerturbRng`).

The "one scripted battle per route" clause of the S9 gate is the traversal harness's job (ZM-74); this ticket makes the table it draws from correct and complete. Species choice: every species at rarity COMMON or UNCOMMON in GDD §5 whose family fits the route's biome should appear on at least one S9 or S10 route, tracked by a unit that reports (not asserts) uncovered species so the S10 tables (ZM-91) can finish the set.
