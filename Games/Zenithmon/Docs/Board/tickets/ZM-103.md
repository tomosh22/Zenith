## Goal
`Roadmap.md` S10 region wave: **Highcrown Pass (Victory Road) and Zenith Gate** (GDD §2.2 indices 34, 12). Author the scenes, their terrain, their buildings, NPCs, trainers and seams from the data the pure tickets froze, and prove them with one traversal row. Content is `ZM_WorldSpec` rows plus recipes, not bespoke code.

**Terrain.** One recipe per outdoor scene in `Source/World/ZM_TerrainAuthoring.cpp`, sized to its content (terrain size and density are per-terrain since `5819ef45`; Dawnmere is 4x4 chunks at 256 m, Route 1 is a corridor), with slot materials from the shared engine sets (ZM-D-215/216) and per-recipe biome character from GDD §2.2: Highcrown Pass is a cave-and-summit gauntlet of tunnels, ledges and the final open climb with the strongest wilds in the region; Zenith Gate is the League summit citadel at the crown of Mount Culmen. Read `Docs/MapLayoutPlaybook.md` §1 and §2 before the first dab: a layout is a fixed point you iterate to, `Flatten` is a rate and `SetHeight` an assignment (§3.1), pads for anything a body stands on. Bake once warm; `uZM_*_REQUIRED_OUTPUT_COUNT` pinned.

**Scenes.** Each outdoor scene: terrain host with `ZM_TerrainGrass` and, for ROUTES ONLY, `ZM_TallGrassSystem` (ZM-D-205, never in the shared helper), player and camera, seam gates and arrival markers per the WorldSpec connections (payload needles per gate, ZM-D-203), the town's buildings as facade-plus-collider entities from a `*Facades.h` table with civic doors into the shared templates (ZM-75), NPC and trainer entities at the stations the pure tickets declared, a `*Dressing.{h,cpp}` module (legs, keep-out, scatter, tree clumps vs hard clearance). No gym. Zenith Gate carries a Care Center, the League Halls door (ZM-38 authors that interior) and the Battle Tower door (ZM-41); the League receptionist and the eight-badge gate are rows from ZM-93, and the steps outside the League doors are where ZM-39 later places the final rival battle. Placement headers hold every coordinate as a measured constant; every new column is frozen by a `Tests/ZM_AutoTests_RouteGround.cpp` oracle; registry rows in `ZM_SceneRegistry.h` land in THIS commit with the files (the black-screen hazard).

**Tests.** Committed-bytes needles for every new entity, camera-clearance samples per new leg (ZM-25), the traversal row `ZM_Traversal_J_Test` (ZM-74) walking every edge and winning one scripted battle per route, and the bake-manifest stamps.

**Why `needs-gpu`:** Zenithmon defers scene authoring on Null (`sceneAuthoring=DEFERRED`) and the bake needs a device. ZM-D-190 order: both configs, authoring boot on `Vulkan_vs2022_Debug_Win64_True` with `--skip-unit-tests`, `git status` the WHOLE `Assets/` tree (a Vulkan boot authors every scene; every previously committed scene must come back byte-identical), a second boot proving byte-identity, then the gate on `Null_`.
