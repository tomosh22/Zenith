## Goal

`ZM-D-203` §5 records a scoped deviation from Zenithmon's one-column-per-anchor rule and names
the work owed to close it. R1-3 authored `DawnmereNorthGate` at `(512, 876)` but could not give
it its own measured ground row, so `ZM_GetDawnmereNorthGateCentreY()` derives the box centre
from the `FromRoute1` arrival column 12 m to the south.

The decision log states the reason plainly: a route-seam row must be a real downward raycast
against the baked Dawnmere heightfield, an unmeasured row ships as a placeholder that
`ZM_DawnmereRouteSeamGroundTruth_Test` reds on at 0.15 m tolerance, and the table is pinned at
exactly one row by `ZM_Interaction/RouteSeamGround_StandsOnTheFromRoute1LandmarkAndIsMeasured`
— which sat outside R1-3's file list.

The measured 12 m deltas on the other two regions show the ground genuinely moves over that
distance: Thornacre's two columns differ by 0.254 m, Route 1's south pair by 0.475 m and its
north pair by 0.962 m. Dawnmere's gate is therefore seated on a neighbouring column's height
rather than its own.

**Why this is worth a ticket rather than a comment.** ZM-D-203's Decision 1 argues that a rule
living only in prose is what lets a seam ship half-built. The follow-up is currently recorded
only in prose — in the decision log and in a comment block in
`Games/Zenithmon/Source/World/ZM_DawnmerePlacement.h`. Nothing fails while it is undone, and
the deviation removed the only thing that could detect it: nothing raycasts `(512, 876)`, and
`ZM_SeamRoundTrip_Test` skips without the gitignored terrain bakes, so it is a pass on CI.

**The bound is currently safe, and the reasoning for it is not quite right.** R1-3 states the
cost as "~1 m out of plumb against a 1.8 m body, so the sensor still spans the walked capsule
from below its feet to well over its head". That holds only when the gate column's ground sits
at or above the arrival column's. If the gate column were 1.8 m lower there would be no overlap
at all and the sensor would never fire. The expected sign here is favourable — the gate at
`(512, 876)` lies inside the RouteGate pad (30 m flatten radius) *and* 3.71 m from the Route
path (18 m radius), while the arrival at `(512, 864)` is only in the path corridor — so the
error is expected around −0.37 m and the sensor is safe today. The sentence should be corrected
along with the measurement.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] `(512, 876)` is measured against a warm Dawnmere bake and carries its own route-seam row,
      OBSERVED from a real raycast rather than derived
- [ ] `ZM_GetDawnmereNorthGateCentreY()` reads that row instead of the `FromRoute1` column

- [ ] Every affected game's pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED run
