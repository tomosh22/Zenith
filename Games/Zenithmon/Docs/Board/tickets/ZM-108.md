## Goal
GDD §8 post-game: *"Zenaris/Nadirath become approachable (Highcrown summit / Route 10 bog depths); seeing both wakes Equinara roaming route grass."* GDD §5 has the three LEGENDARY rows (`ZM_SPECIES_ZENARIS` the Noon Crown, `ZM_SPECIES_NADIRATH` the Under-Coil, `ZM_SPECIES_EQUINARA` the Horizon-Walker) at L60-70, and the rarity tier's stated purpose is to gate the post-game.

**A Scope §4 ruling is required before implementation, which is why this carries `question` and `deferred`.** `Scope.md` §1 lists the post-game as *"Champion rematch + Battle Tower"* and nothing else; the GDD, which "details" Scope, adds the legendaries. No agent may widen scope on its own judgement; the user records an `ZM-D-` entry either adding the three encounters to Scope §1 or cutting them (in which case the three species stay in the dex as unobtainable and this ticket closes WONT_DO).

If ruled IN: three stationary encounter entities (`ZM_EncounterZone`-style interactable that starts a wild battle with a fixed species and level, `m_bCanCatch = true`, one attempt persisted by a story flag per legendary, re-approachable on a faint until caught), placed by a Highcrown summit and a Route 10 bog-centre re-author; Equinara as a roaming encounter that joins every route's roll at a tiny weight once both flags are set (a runtime augmentation of `RollStepForScene`, never a table edit, so the tables stay deterministic). Proof: headless units for the flag gating and the roaming augmentation with an anti-vacuity arm; an automated test that catches Zenaris through the real HUD.
