## Goal
ZM-D-168 visual audit item 4, the half of ZM-58 a machine can do: put the **Trade Post and the Care Center** into `Dawnmere.zscen` as real buildings. Today the town has a shop clerk NPC (`Npc_TradePostClerk`, `Source/World/ZM_DawnmerePlacement.cpp`), a Care Center heal flow (`Source/CareCenter/ZM_CareCenter.cpp`) and roster rows for both buildings with real footprints (`Source/Data/ZM_BuildingData.cpp`), and neither building stands anywhere.

**RE-SCOPED 2026-09-19: the mechanism now exists, use it.** ZM-D-219 (2026-08-30) gave Dawnmere its Home and Lab as real generated architecture: `ZM_BuildingGen` v3 emits wall/roof/trim/glass surface meshes with four-map PBR sets for all 30 sets, and a placed building is a set of blockout COLLIDER entities (kept, because every clearance constant and the camera clamp were measured against them) plus one `ZM_BuildingFacade` entity that resolves its building from the entity name through `Source/World/ZM_DawnmereFacades.h` and loads the model at RUNTIME. The model is deliberately NOT authored into the scene (`Zenith_ModelComponent::WriteToDataStream` inlines materials and made two boots differ by 3 KB). Follow that exact shape for the two new buildings: two rows in `ZM_DawnmereFacades.h`, blockouts and a facade entity each, door apertures sized from the roster's `m_fDoorWidth`/`m_fDoorHeight`, and `m_bSiteFixed` so the jitter draw is consumed but suppressed.

**Constraints:**
- Dawnmere is v8 (ZM-D-218): 4x4 chunks, 256 m, Home-to-Lab 56 m. Read `Docs/MapLayoutPlaybook.md` §1 and §2 FIRST; a placement is a fixed point you iterate to. Place inside the recipe's reserved pads (`Plaza`, `Market`) without editing the recipe: any pad, path or landmark edit regenerates the whole heightmap and re-measures every table in `ZM_DawnmerePlacement.h`.
- Both footprints must clear `ZM_DawnmereDressing.h`'s drive-leg table and the trainer sight radius (playbook §3.5, §3.6), and the scatter keep-out must learn the two new footprints so no rock or bush stands in a doorway.
- Their coordinates are compiled constants in `Source/World/ZM_DawnmerePlacement.h` in the measured-table idiom, each on its own FROZEN ground column from the `ZM_DawnmereHomeGroundTruth_Test` oracle, never a neighbour's height.
- The interiors (Care Center 52, Trade Post 53) are NOT this ticket (ZM-75); the doors here are visual until then, and the clerk and caretaker keep their outdoor stations.
- `EveryGameComponentIsInBothRegistries` must stay green; ZM-D-219's finding was a component in one registry authoring a scene silently without it.

**Why `needs-gpu`:** Zenithmon defers scene authoring on Null (`sceneAuthoring=DEFERRED`); ZM-D-190 order, `sceneAuthoring=AUTHOR_DAWNMERE`, `--skip-unit-tests`, two byte-identical boots, `git status` the whole `Assets/` tree.
