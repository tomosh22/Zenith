## Goal
GDD §3.3 and §4: *"Vesper picks the starter strong against the player's"*, six battles in total, teams anchored on the counter-starter family. GDD §4 records this as **DESIGN INTENT, NOT SHIPPED**: `ZM_StarterChoice` carries `m_eCounteredBy` and `ZM_ResolveCounterStarterSpecies` exists (`Source/Party/ZM_StarterChoice.{h,cpp}`) but nothing consumes it, and Vesper's row in `Source/Data/ZM_TrainerData.cpp` is a fixed L5 Kindlet, booked as debt against S8 in Shortfalls §1.8. Every later rival battle inherits the bug, so it is fixed before any of them is authored.

Scope, PURE:
- A trainer row may declare a party slot as `ZM_TRAINER_PARTY_COUNTER_STARTER` at a given evolution stage; `ZM_GetTrainerData` resolves it against the saved `ZM_STARTER_CHOICE` through `ZM_ResolveCounterStarterSpecies` at battle-build time (the by-row seam and `ZM_TrainerBattle` already build the enemy party from the row). A player with no recorded choice (pre-lab save) fails CLOSED to the row's literal species, and a unit pins that.
- Vesper's six rows (GDD §8): Route 1 L5 (the existing row, re-pointed to the counter starter), Tidegate docks ~L21, Milldown ~L30, Route 11 snowline ~L40, Highcrown gate ~L50, Zenith Gate steps ~L54; each with its own `ZM_STORY_FLAG_RIVAL__N___DEFEATED`, escalating `ZM_AI_TIER`, challenge and defeat lines, and a party that grows by one member per beat with the counter family evolving at the GDD §4 stages. Only the Route 1 row is placed today; ZM-82 relocates it and ZM-39 places the other five.
- `Tests/ZM_Tests_TrainerData.cpp`, `ZM_Tests_StarterChoice.cpp` and the rival tests (`ZM_AutoTests_RivalVesper.cpp`, `ZM_Tests_IntroBeat.cpp`) updated so a Fernfawn pick meets a Kindlet, a Kindlet pick meets a Finlet, a Finlet pick meets a Fernfawn, proven for all three picks headless and once windowed.

No save-schema change: the starter choice is already persisted and the resolution is derived at battle time.
