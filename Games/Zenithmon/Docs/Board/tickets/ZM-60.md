## Goal
Open visual debt from ZM-D-168 and Shortfalls 1.8-3: the trainer "spotted" beat has no camera cut. ZM-D-159 gave the beat a 0.35 s `ZM_TRAINER_SIGHT_SPOTTED` window with an exclamation mark drawn from `Flux_Primitives`; the camera keeps following the player through it, so the moment reads as a HUD event rather than a scene.

**The blocker once recorded here was DISPROVED** (Shortfalls 1.8-3, corrected 2026-08-01): `ZM_FollowCamera` (Zenithmon component, order 103, `Components/ZM_FollowCamera.{h,cpp}`) is the SOLE writer of the camera pose and owns `OnLateUpdate`, so the override belongs INSIDE that component and needs no `Zenith/` change. Build a small override stack on `ZM_FollowCamera`: during SPOTTED, ease the pivot toward the midpoint between player and trainer and tighten the arm, hold for the window, then ease back over the approach walk (ZM-D-163/166/167). The occlusion raycast and the clamped-arm contract (`ZM_DawnmereCameraClearance_Test`, at least 50% of the authored pivot-to-camera distance) must still hold at every sample during the cut, so the override must pass through `ClampArmDistance`, never bypass it. The cut is cancelled when the sighting cancels (player walks out of the cone), the same way the exclamation mark is.

Prove it headless where it can be proven: a unit over the pure easing (start, hold, end poses; cancel mid-way returns to follow without a snap) and an arm on `ZM_AutoTests_TrainerSight.cpp` that reads the camera pose during SPOTTED. The visual judgement of the cut itself is the `human-gate` half: this ticket stops at In Review with a capture attached.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A camera cut plays during `ZM_TRAINER_SIGHT_SPOTTED` and eases back during the approach, implemented inside `ZM_FollowCamera` with no engine change
- [ ] The clamped-arm contract holds at every frame of the cut on the real Dawnmere scene, asserted by an automated test
- [ ] Cancelling the sighting cancels the cut without a snap, proven by a headless unit over the pure easing
- [ ] A capture is attached and a human visual sign-off is recorded before Done
- [ ] Every affected game's pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED run
