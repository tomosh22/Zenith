## Goal
Shortfalls §1.6, still OPEN from the S6 dialogue box: *"the 8-line cap is cumulative per conversation, not per pending line. The queue never compacts as lines are consumed, so `QueueLine` while active cannot be used to chunk a conversation past 8 total lines. Fine for NPC barks; if long conversations arrive, compact on advance or raise the cap."* S9 brings the first long conversations (gym leaders' challenge and defeat speeches, Aster's Tidegate check-in, the daycare couple), so this lands before ZM-77 authors them.

Scope: `Source/UI/ZM_UI_DialogueBox` compacts consumed lines on advance so the cap bounds PENDING lines, not the conversation; `ZM_UI_MenuStack::PushDialogueLines` and `QueueLine` keep their signatures; the armed-choice mode (ZM-D-121) and the single-tenant rule (an unanswered question owns the box) are unchanged. Headless units in `Tests/ZM_Tests_DialogueBox.cpp`: a 20-line conversation plays every line in order through real confirm edges, the cap still refuses a burst larger than the pending budget, and the choice-mode and refusal units stay green. One existing windowed test (`ZM_NpcTalk_Test`) gains a long-conversation row so the compaction is exercised through real input once.
