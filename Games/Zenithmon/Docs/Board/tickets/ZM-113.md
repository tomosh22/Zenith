## Goal
Shortfalls §2, E8 TIER 2, still DEFERRED after TIER 1 landed in ZM-49: *"TIER 2 keeps or loads the heightfield and would make the query work at AUTHORING time."* `Zenith_TerrainComponent::TryGetGroundHeightAt` answers from the physics mesh at RUNTIME, which is why every Zenithmon placement column is measured by a downward raycast in an automated oracle after a boot (`Tests/ZM_AutoTests_RouteGround.cpp`, the four ground-truth oracles in `MapLayoutPlaybook.md` §4) and frozen into the header by hand. Twenty-plus new outdoor scenes in S9 and S10 (ZM-84 to ZM-103) each repeat that loop.

Scope, engine side (`Zenith/**`, so the gate union builds and tests every game and the cross-game sweep applies): the terrain bake writes, or the component can load, the quantised heightfield the bake was produced from (the per-terrain dimensions travel with it since `5819ef45`); a `Zenith_TerrainComponent::TryGetBakedGroundHeightAt(x, z)` that answers from the heightfield with no physics world; an editor-automation step that resolves a placement's Y from it during scene authoring so a header constant can be DERIVED at authoring time and the raycast oracle becomes a cross-check rather than the source. Units: the heightfield query agrees with the physics-mesh query to the oracle tolerance at every sample of one committed scene; the query is total off-terrain; RenderTest's `TerrainEditorSmoke` still passes.

It stays a Zenithmon-board ticket under ZM-14 like E6/E7/E8, so its gates are the Zenithmon list plus the Engine union.
