## Goal

Questions.md **Q-2026-08-14-001 [OPEN]**.

`GraphComponent::ThousandEntityUpdateBenchmark` is an ENGINE wall-clock assertion — `ZENITH_ASSERT_LT(msPerFrame, 5.0)` over 1000 entities, `Zenith_GraphComponent.Tests.inl:1555`. It measured **6.684 ms** while the box was building and booting concurrently, and passed on a quiet re-run with nothing else changed.

The gate is otherwise an exact-equality determinism ratchet and `zm-tests` is a REQUIRED check, so this one unit makes a required check load-dependent **for every game**.

**A red naming that benchmark is a FLAKE to re-run quiet, not a regression.** The fix is engine-side — this ticket is filed against the ENGINE board on purpose.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] The assertion no longer depends on wall-clock time under load, or is moved out of the gate
- [ ] Questions.md Q-2026-08-14-001 updated with the resolution
