## Goal
`Roadmap.md` S9: *"Daycare / breeding / eggs (step counting on the manager)"*. The breeding MODEL is feature-complete (ZM-D-048/049/050: gender, egg groups, GLOOPET, egg moves, ability inheritance, hatch cycles) and `Source/Battle/ZM_Daycare.{h,cpp}` already has `ZM_DaycareState` (two slots, `m_uEggStepCounter`, `m_bEggAvailable`), `ZM_DaycareStep(state, steps)` and deposit/withdraw; `ZM_SaveSchema` persists daycare parents, egg and aggregate hatch progress since S7. What is missing is the SURFACE over it, which this ticket builds PURE and headless-provable; the Milldown daycare interior and couple are authored by the S10 wave (ZM-98).

Scope:
- **Step counting on the manager.** `ZM_GameStateManager` (order 104) already counts tile transitions for the encounter roll; feed the same transitions to `ZM_DaycareStep` and to every egg in the party (hatch cycles), so an egg hatches after the derived step count. Hatch is a beat: a dialogue line and the species reveal, no new UI screen.
- **Deposit / withdraw flow** as a dialogue-choice conversation in the S6 `ZM_UI_MenuStack` armed-choice idiom (the Care Center heal is the precedent, ZM-D-121): pick a party member to leave, pay to collect, take the egg when `m_bEggAvailable`. An NPC role `ZM_NPC_ROLE_DAYCARE` on `ZM_NpcData` routes to it, so the couple in S10 are data rows, not code.
- **Egg in the party.** An egg occupies a party slot, cannot battle or be switched in, and shows as an egg in `ZM_UI_Party`; the battle engine already refuses an egg-shaped monster or must be taught to (unit).
- **Route 7 is the egg-hatching circuit** (GDD §2.2): nothing here is route-specific, but the hatch cycles should be tuned so one lap of Route 7 hatches a common egg; record the derivation.

Headless proof throughout: units over `ZM_DaycareStep` integration, an automated test with a test-authored daycare NPC in an existing scene that deposits two compatible parents, walks the tile transitions, collects and hatches. No save-schema change is expected (state is persisted already); if one is needed it is a version bump with a canned-blob test per `SaveFormat.md`.

- [ ] The hatch-cycle tuning for one Route 7 lap is derived and recorded in `Docs/GameDesignDocument.md` §8
