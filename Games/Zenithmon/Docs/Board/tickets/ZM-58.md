## Goal
ZM-D-168 visual audit, item 4: *"Dawnmere reads as an open field, not a town"*. Since the audit, Dawnmere has been compacted twice (ZM-D-217/218: 6x6 then 4x4 chunks, Home-to-Lab 56 m, about 770 instanced props and 13 tree clumps from `ZM_DawnmereDressing`), and its Home and Lab are real generated architecture (ZM-D-219). What is still missing is the Trade Post and the Care Center as standing buildings, which is ZM-71 and BLOCKS this ticket.

**This ticket is the SIGN-OFF only.** A person boots `Vulkan_vs2022_Debug_Win64_True`, walks the town from the spawn to the route gate, and records whether it reads as a village: density, silhouette, the two civic buildings legible as shop and clinic, nothing floating or z-fighting. The verdict goes into `DecisionLog.md` as a `ZM-D-` entry that closes audit item 4, or lists the remaining defects as tickets under ZM-18.

`needs-human` because of the DELIVERABLE, not the engine: a visual judgement is a person's and no runner produces one. `ZM_PhotoTour_Test` (manual-only) can capture the reference frames to attach.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A person has walked Dawnmere on a windowed build after ZM-71 and recorded the verdict as a `ZM-D-` entry closing ZM-D-168 item 4, or filed the remaining defects under ZM-18
