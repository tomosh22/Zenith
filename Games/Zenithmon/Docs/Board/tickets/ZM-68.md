## Goal

`Npc_AuthoredAppearancesAreMutuallyDistinct` requires 0.15 RGB separation between
**every pair** of NPC rows naming different `ZM_HUMAN_ID`s. That is stricter than
the property it exists to protect, and the gap is now load-bearing rather than
theoretical: it exhausted the palette and blocked ZM-23 (R1-5).

★ **THE INVARIANT GUARDS A PREVIEW COLOUR, NOT PLAYER-VISIBLE DISTINCTNESS.**
`ZM_GetHumanPaletteColour` (`Source/Gen/ZM_HumanAppearance.cpp:824`) is
`0.45 * outfit.primary + 0.25 * outfit.accent + 0.30 * hair`. It reads NEITHER
skin tone, build, attachment NOR hair *style* — all four of which a rendered
human actually differs by. So two NPCs measuring 0.13 apart on this scalar can be
obviously different people on screen, and the invariant reds anyway.

★ **AND IT IS GLOBAL WHERE THE PROPERTY IS LOCAL.** Two Route 1 trainers can
never share a frame with a Dawnmere townsperson — different scenes, reachable
only through a warp with a full fade. Requiring them to differ buys nothing a
player could observe, while consuming cells from a 36-cell space
(6 non-themed outfits x 6 non-DYED hairs).

**Ruling C was taken for R1-5** (ZM-D-208) — append an outfit and/or hair slot — because it
moves no existing colour and unblocked the chain immediately. It is a REFILL, not
a fix: one outfit slot yields at most 6 candidate cells against a floor every new
colour must still clear, and S9's "Towns 3-6, Routes 2-8, Gyms 2-4" wants far
more NPCs than that. This ticket is the structural half.

**What the fix needs, and why it was not just done:** re-scoping to "rows that can
share a frame" requires knowing which scene an NPC belongs to, and `ZM_NpcData`
carries no scene column. That is the whole reason option D was recorded as
blocked rather than chosen.

★ **THE FAILURE MODE TO DESIGN AGAINST IS A VACUOUS INVARIANT.** A re-scoping that
partitions rows too finely stops constraining anything — and unlike the current
state, nothing would red to tell you. Two NPCs in different scenes TODAY may share
one later (the Warden is already scheduled to relocate onto Route 1 by R1-10), so
the partition must follow the compiled world data rather than a hand-maintained
list, and a row reachable from two scenes must be constrained against both.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] An NPC row's scene membership is derivable from compiled data rather than a
      hand-maintained list, so a relocated NPC re-partitions automatically
- [ ] The distinctness invariant constrains every pair of rows that can appear in
      one frame, and a row reachable from more than one scene is constrained
      against every scene it can reach
- [ ] The invariant is proven NON-VACUOUS: a deliberately-colliding pair inside a
      single scene reds it, exercised as an anti-vacuity arm in the test itself
- [ ] The usable-headroom figure is reported from the shipped tables rather than
      asserted, so the next slice can see how much room it actually has
- [ ] Every affected game's pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED run
