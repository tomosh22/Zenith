## Goal
`Roadmap.md` S10 region wave: **Stonereach City, Routes 13 and 14 and the Gym 8 interior** (GDD §2.2 indices 11, 32, 33, 49). Author the scenes, their terrain, their buildings, NPCs, trainers and seams from the data the pure tickets froze, and prove them with one traversal row. Content is `ZM_WorldSpec` rows plus recipes, not bespoke code.

**Terrain.** One recipe per outdoor scene in `Source/World/ZM_TerrainAuthoring.cpp`, sized to its content (terrain size and density are per-terrain since `5819ef45`; Dawnmere is 4x4 chunks at 256 m, Route 1 is a corridor), with slot materials from the shared engine sets (ZM-D-215/216) and per-recipe biome character from GDD §2.2: Stonereach is a canyon city built into badlands cliff faces; Route 13 is a wind-scoured saddle pass of tussock between crags; Route 14 is dry red-grass benches on the canyon floor (battle weather SAND). Read `Docs/MapLayoutPlaybook.md` §1 and §2 before the first dab: a layout is a fixed point you iterate to, `Flatten` is a rate and `SetHeight` an assignment (§3.1), pads for anything a body stands on. Bake once warm; `uZM_*_REQUIRED_OUTPUT_COUNT` pinned.

**Scenes.** Each outdoor scene: terrain host with `ZM_TerrainGrass` and, for ROUTES ONLY, `ZM_TallGrassSystem` (ZM-D-205, never in the shared helper), player and camera, seam gates and arrival markers per the WorldSpec connections (payload needles per gate, ZM-D-203), the town's buildings as facade-plus-collider entities from a `*Facades.h` table with civic doors into the shared templates (ZM-75), NPC and trainer entities at the stations the pure tickets declared, a `*Dressing.{h,cpp}` module (legs, keep-out, scatter, tree clumps vs hard clearance). The Gym 8 interior is authored from `ZM_Gym8Placement.h` (ZM-97) with its door in the town and the leader at the declared station; its registry row lands here. Placement headers hold every coordinate as a measured constant; every new column is frozen by a `Tests/ZM_AutoTests_RouteGround.cpp` oracle; registry rows in `ZM_SceneRegistry.h` land in THIS commit with the files (the black-screen hazard).

**Tests.** Committed-bytes needles for every new entity, camera-clearance samples per new leg (ZM-25), the traversal row `ZM_Traversal_I_Test` (ZM-74) walking every edge and winning one scripted battle per route, and the bake-manifest stamps.

**Why `needs-gpu`:** Zenithmon defers scene authoring on Null (`sceneAuthoring=DEFERRED`) and the bake needs a device. ZM-D-190 order: both configs, authoring boot on `Vulkan_vs2022_Debug_Win64_True` with `--skip-unit-tests`, `git status` the WHOLE `Assets/` tree (a Vulkan boot authors every scene; every previously committed scene must come back byte-identical), a second boot proving byte-identity, then the gate on `Null_`.
