## Goal
Shortfalls §1.2, the three tracked battle-mechanics deferrals, *"faithfully noted, not bugs"*, each recorded with the condition under which it lands. S11's balance pass (ZM-42) measures the final mechanics, so these land before it:

1. **Conditional ball bonuses (SC6):** the net/dusk/quick/heal orbs use their base catch parameter (all x1.0); the type/time/turn/heal conditional multipliers are not computed. With the item-use path real (ZM-33) and turn count available from the engine, compute them in `Source/Battle/ZM_CatchCalc` from the ball's `ZM_ITEM_EFFECT_CATCH` row and the battle state; time of day does not exist in this game (no clock), so the dusk orb's condition is recorded as always-false or re-designed with a `ZM-D-` entry.
2. **High-crit MOVE flag under-applied:** a move's inherent `m_uCritStage == 1` still crits at 1/24 rather than 1/8 because the two scales differ (move `2` = guaranteed vs counter `2` = 1/2). Reconcile the scales explicitly (a mapping function with a totality unit) and fold the move flag into the `RAISE_CRIT` counter path.
3. **Self-targeting damaging-secondary dropped on a KO:** the one self-buff secondary (Primeval Might, `RAISE_ALL` 10%) gates its proc on the defender being alive per ZM-D-033, so a KO drops the user's boost. Decide mainline behaviour (self-target secondaries proc regardless), record the ruling as an amendment to ZM-D-033, and implement.

Every change re-records the affected golden event streams in `Tests/ZM_Tests_Battle.cpp` with the reason in the commit; the 2,000-battle fuzz soak stays green; `ZM_ValidateEventStream` rules are extended, not relaxed. Q-2026-07-12-001 (rule 7 and WEATHER_DAMAGE/ability-chip faints) is the same shape and closes here too.
