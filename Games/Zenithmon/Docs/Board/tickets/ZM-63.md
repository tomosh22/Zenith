## Goal

`ZM_InteriorTintPixels_Test` fails on a **reproducible, diagnosed** shortfall: PlayerHome's interior tint is too weak to clear the asserted 0.15 red/blue separation floor.

Measured windowed on an RTX 2060, 2026-08-21, against a fresh `Vulkan_vs2022_Debug_Win64_True` build:

| | first-run calibration | ZM-D-177 | **today** |
|---|---|---|---|
| PlayerHome red/blue | 1.3045 | 1.1888 | **1.1898** |
| ProfLab red/blue | 1.0742 | 1.0684 | **1.0686** |
| gap (floor 0.15) | 0.2303 | 0.1204 | **0.1212** |

Deltas of ~0.001 against ZM-D-177's numbers: this is **stable and reproducible**, not a flake. That closes the open question ZM-D-177 left as *"the cause is unattributed — recorded here as deferred work requiring a windowed re-examination."*

**It also rules out all three causes the test itself enumerates.** The gap is not ~0 (the tint DID reach the framebuffer), both absolutes are not high (it did NOT leak into ProfLab — ProfLab is level at 1.0686 vs 1.0742 calibration), and the gap is not negative (the bedroom is correctly the warmer room). What remains: **PlayerHome's tint fell ~0.116 from calibration on its own.**

So the tint is present and correctly signed, merely too weak. The fix is to strengthen PlayerHome's interior tint until the rendered separation clears 0.15 with margin.

**This is WINDOWED work and cannot be done by the agent loop.** It re-authors `Assets/Scenes/PlayerHome.zscen`, and a headless run may CREATE a `.zscen` but never CHANGE one — the deliverable would be silently missing while every unit stayed green.

**Do NOT shrink `fPT_MIN_RATIO_MARGIN`.** The test says so in its own failure message: *"If the two bands OVERLAP, the suite cannot see the tint on pixels: RECORD THAT, do NOT shrink the floor until it passes."* 0.15 is the ZM-D-176 ruling's threshold, not a tuning knob, and lowering it buys a green gate by deleting the property under test.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] PlayerHome's tint strengthened so the rendered red/blue gap clears 0.15 with margin
- [ ] `ZM_InteriorTintPixels_Test` passes on a windowed `Vulkan_` run, with the observed numbers recorded
- [ ] `fPT_MIN_RATIO_MARGIN` unchanged at 0.15
- [ ] ProfLab's absolute stays near 1.07 — i.e. the tint was strengthened, not leaked into both rooms

## Comments

**Closed 2026-09-19 by the board audit: RESOLVED by ZM-D-220 (2026-08-30).** PlayerHome's tint was weak because both interiors were the same grey blockout box with one per-scene vertex hue nudge. ZM-D-220 rebuilt both rooms as generated materials under lights (timber boards and warm plaster against resin tile and cool panels), and the measured gap became 0.7449 against the unmoved 0.15 floor, with ProfLab at 0.7809. Every DoD clause holds: the tint was strengthened by construction, `fPT_MIN_RATIO_MARGIN` is still 0.15, and the test is green on the windowed run recorded in ZM-D-220 and ZM-D-221.
