## Goal

The three Route 1 ground-item props authored by ZM-27 (ZM-D-207) render as
`ZM_GreyboxVisual` blockouts — the engine unit cube with a flat blockout colour,
0.6 m on a side. They read as placeholders because they are placeholders. Bring
them to the visual standard the rest of the game is held to.

Zenithmon generates and bakes all art procedurally from tools builds
(`Source/Gen/` is the existing pattern — see `ZM_BuildingGen` and
`ZM_HumanAppearance`), so this is a generator plus a material, not an imported
asset.

**Scope is the presentation only.** The three props are
`ZM_GROUND_ITEM_ROUTE1_SOUTH_SALVE`, `ZM_GROUND_ITEM_ROUTE1_LANE_CATCHORB` and
`ZM_GROUND_ITEM_ROUTE1_NORTH_SALVE`. The pickup rule
(`ZM_CanPickUpGroundItem` / `ZM_TryPickUpGroundItem`), the prop registry, the
`ZM_GroundItemProp` component contract and save module 12 all shipped in ZM-27
and must not change.

★ **THE MEASURED ANCHORS ARE FROZEN AND THE REACH BUDGET IS TIGHT.** Each prop
stands on its own measured ground column, and
`Route1_GroundItemPropsAreReachableFromTheWalkedLane` holds them inside
`fZM_INTERACT_MAX_DISTANCE` (2.5 m) of the walked lane, with perpendicular
offsets of 1.60 / 2.00 / 1.75 m. A visual pass that makes a prop physically
larger must keep its interact origin where it is — growing the mesh is fine,
moving the anchor is not, and a prop nudged off the lane can never be picked up
in any playthrough while every other check stays green.

★ **AND A PROP HAS NO COLLIDER, DELIBERATELY** (ZM-D-207). Adding one would put
an obstacle in the walked lane and a SOLID body over the prop's own ground
column, which `ZM_Route1GroundTruth_Test` treats as a failure rather than a
filter. Presentation must not introduce one.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Each of the three props renders with a purpose-built mesh and material
      rather than the `ZM_GreyboxVisual` blockout cube
- [ ] The two item kinds are distinguishable from each other on sight at the
      distance a player walks past them, not only in a close-up
- [ ] A prop the current save has already collected does not still read as a
      pickup — its presentation matches the inert state
      `ZM_GroundItemProp::IsInteractable()` already reports
- [ ] Each prop still sits on its own measured ground column with its interact
      origin unmoved, and no prop gains a collider
- [ ] If authored transforms change, `Route1.zscen` is re-authored from a
      windowed `Vulkan_*_True` boot and proven reproducible by two consecutive
      boots producing byte-identical output
- [ ] Every affected game's pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED run
- [ ] Before/after captures of all three props recorded in the work log, so the
      visual sign-off has something to judge
