## Goal
TDD §8 and GDD §5.5. Poles (small/medium/large) union by wire reach on placement; machines map to the covering pole's root; removal marks the network dirty and an end-of-tick BFS over the pole graph rebuilds it. Per network per tick: production capacity and demand maintained incrementally (never rescanned) → `satisfaction = min(1, supply/demand)` in Q16.16 → consumers scale by it (brownout: everything slows, nothing hard-stops). Accumulator priority solar → accumulator → steam resolved in the same pass (solar/accumulator archetypes land in M1-08). "Stats rings designed in now": per network, 64-sample rings at tick/second/minute cadence of production and consumption per category. Multiple isolated networks are legal. Phase 3 of the tick order.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] Two pole clusters joined by one pole become one network; removing that pole splits them at end of tick with per-side satisfaction recomputed (unit)
- [ ] Demand 2× supply gives every consumer satisfaction exactly 0.5 in Q16.16 and halves craft progress per tick (pinned)
- [ ] Satisfaction changes wake no sleepers (`uMachinesWoken` pinned at 0 across a brownout transition); a full network-off transition does wake them
- [ ] Stats rings hold the last 64 samples at each cadence and the minute ring is the mean of its seconds
- [ ] Foundry pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED Null run
