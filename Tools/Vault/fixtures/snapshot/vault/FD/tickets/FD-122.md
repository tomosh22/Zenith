## Goal
GDD §10: the factory is the soundtrack — layered machine hum from what is on screen, density-mixed; sparse ambient music under it, ducking under alerts; positional one-shots (placement thunk, inserter tick, train horn, turret fire) attenuated by camera distance/zoom tier; distinct stingers for wave-spawned / first-contact / breach; mix targets mobile speakers first with a headphone-wide option. Author the asset set (ogg, checked in under `Assets/Audio/`, licensed or generated), map every `FD_Sounds.h` name to an asset, and expose the two mix profiles in settings. The final mix is judged by listening, so this stops at In Review.

- [ ] Both mix profiles are selectable and persisted
