## Goal
GDD §5.6: labs consume science packs; one active research at a time; a queue (depth 5) as an early QoL unlock; research unlocks buildings, recipes and capability upgrades. `FD_Research` state: current tech, progress in pack units, unlocked bitset, queue. `StartResearch{tech}` / `QueueResearch` commands. Unlock gating: `PlaceBuilding`/`SetRecipe` refuse a locked archetype/recipe with a reason code; splitter priority/filter (M0-09) become researched toggles. Red-tier tree content (~12 techs) in `FD_Techs` with prerequisites. Lab archetype consumes one pack of each required type per research unit, via the timer wheel.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A lab fed red packs completes the first tech after exactly the table pack count (pin), and the unlocked archetype becomes placeable on that tick
- [ ] Starting a tech whose prerequisite is locked is refused with a reason code
- [ ] The queue advances automatically and is capped at 5 until its own unlock research completes
- [ ] Research state round-trips through the digest (state is part of the world digest)
- [ ] Foundry pinned baseline bumped from an OBSERVED Null run
