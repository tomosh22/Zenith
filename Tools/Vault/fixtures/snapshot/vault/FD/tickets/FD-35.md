## Goal
GDD §5.5: burner boot → steam (offshore pump → boiler → steam engine) → solar + accumulator. Full fluid networks arrive in M3a (TDD §9); for M1 the boiler consumes water from an adjacent offshore pump and the engine consumes steam from an adjacent boiler through a **rate-limited direct connection** (`FD_FluidEndpoint` with per-tick Q16.16 caps — the same endpoint type M3a attaches to networks, so nothing is thrown away). Boiler burns fuel via the timer wheel; engine contributes production capacity while steam is available. Solar contributes by a fixed day fraction (no day/night cycle in v1 — record as `FD-D-` decision); accumulators charge/discharge with the §8 priority.
